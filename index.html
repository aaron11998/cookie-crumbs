Make your own tip page

Any Cookie Chain address gets a shareable tip page — same app, your jar, zero setup. Perfect for creators, bots, and community funds. The 0.75% protocol fee on each tip is what keeps the platform running.

How it works

  1. Connect. Nightly or any Solana-standard wallet, pointed at Cookie Chain.
  2. Tip. Your wallet builds a system-program transfer to the jar — you sign, we broadcast.
  3. Confirm. We poll the RPC until your signature is confirmed, then show the receipt + explorer link.
  4. See it. The feed re-reads the jar's history from the chain. Everyone's crumbs, anyone's view.

Cookie Crumbs is 100% client-side. No accounts, no database, no analytics — your connection talks straight to rpc.cookiescan.io. Source: GitHub.
