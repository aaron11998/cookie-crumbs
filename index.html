Cookie Crumbs is a fully on-chain tip jar for the Cookie Chain community. Every tip is a plain system-program transfer — no middleman, no custodian, no backend. Connect with Nightly (or any Solana-standard wallet), attach a message, and it lands on-chain in about a second.

No wallet detected. Install Nightly and switch its network to Cookie Chain (RPC: https://rpc.cookiescan.io). Any Solana-standard injected wallet also works.

Crumb Feed

loading crumbs from the chain…
Feed is read straight from Cookie Chain RPC (getSignaturesForAddress → getTransaction). Messages are posted on-chain via the SPL Memo program and visible to everyone. Tips of 5+ COOK are 🚀 BOOST — they get pinned to the top of the feed, so everyone sees them first.

Make your own tip page

Any Cookie Chain address gets a shareable tip page — same app, your jar, zero setup. Perfect for creators, bots, and community funds. The 0.75% protocol fee on each tip is what keeps the platform running.
